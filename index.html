  • Available request types: Choose the option that best matches your preferred stream goal.
    Play GameA standard gameplay session focused on the selected title.

Important Request Policies

Payment and Approval

  • Payment deadline: Complete payment within 48 hours of staff approval or the request will automatically expire.
  • Final payment: Once payment is submitted, the request is final and cannot be canceled or refunded.
  • Approval discretion: ⁅𝐓𝐡𝐲𝐓☣︎𝐱𝐢𝐜𝐆𝐚𝐦𝐞𝐫⁆ may approve, reschedule, or deny any game request.

Scheduling and Completion

  • Stream window: Paid and approved requests are scheduled as an 18–24 hour stream beginning Friday evening, Saturday morning, or on the day immediately preceding a holiday.
  • Rescheduling: Postponed streams and technical problems are moved to another available date.

VOD Availability

  • YouTube: Completed streams remain permanently available.
  • Twitch: VODs are retained for 60 days.

Request Availability

  • Single active request: Only one viewer request may be active at a time.
  • Closed request slot: Closing the Viewer Request Slot prevents new submissions only. Existing requests remain active through approval, payment, scheduling, and completion.

How to Request a Game

Review these steps, then sign in with Twitch in the account panel directly below to begin your request.

  1. Sign in with TwitchUse the Twitch account panel directly below this guide. You must be signed in before any request can be submitted.
  2. Choose your request pathFor a catalog game, find the title and select Request Game. For an unlisted game, enter its complete title under Request a Game Not Listed and select Choose Request Type.
  3. Select the request typeChoose Play Game, Speed Run Game, or 100% Completion and review the displayed price.
  4. Confirm and submitVerify the game, platform, request type, Twitch account, and optional note before submitting the request for staff review.
  5. Track staff approvalSelect View Request Status to monitor the request. If approved, the secure StreamElements payment option will appear.
  6. Complete paymentSubmit payment within 48 hours of approval. Once payment is confirmed, the request proceeds to scheduling.
