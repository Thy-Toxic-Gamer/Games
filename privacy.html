This notice explains how ⁅𝐓𝐡𝐲𝐓☣︎𝐱𝐢𝐜𝐆𝐚𝐦𝐞𝐫⁆ Game Request System uses information when you browse the catalog, sign in with Twitch, submit or manage a request, or complete an approved payment.

Information Used

What the request system processes

Twitch sign-in

The system uses the Twitch account identifier and Twitch name needed to sign you in, associate you with your own requests, and verify owner or moderator access when applicable.

Game requests

Request records may contain the selected game, console or system, request type, price bracket, optional viewer note, status, timestamps, schedule, completion date, VOD links, cancellation or denial explanation, and eligible game-change information.

Payment confirmation

For an approved request, the system stores a generated request code and limited confirmation details such as provider, currency, amount, payment time, and transaction reference needed to match the payment. The website does not store full card numbers, bank credentials, or PayPal passwords.

Staff and delivery records

Authorized staff verification, review actions, reasons, scheduling changes, and limited Discord delivery logs may be recorded to operate and protect the request system.

Purpose

Why this information is used

  • Authenticate viewers and verified staff.
  • Accept, review, update, schedule, and complete game requests.
  • Match approved payments to the correct request.
  • Send request and system-status notifications to configured Discord channels.
  • Prevent duplicate, unauthorized, or conflicting actions.
  • Maintain an accurate operational and moderation history.

Access and Sharing

Who can receive the information

  • You can view your own request after signing in with the same Twitch account.
  • ⁅𝐓𝐡𝐲𝐓☣︎𝐱𝐢𝐜𝐆𝐚𝐦𝐞𝐫⁆ and currently verified Twitch moderators can access Staff Control.
  • Operational request details may be sent to configured staff Discord channels.
  • After completion, the public website and completed-request Discord channel may display only the game, system, stream type, completed date, and VOD links. Viewer identity, notes, request IDs, and payment information are not included.
  • Service providers process only the information needed for authentication, hosting, database operation, notifications, or payment confirmation.
  • Request information is not sold.

Connected Services

Services with their own privacy terms

The request system relies on third-party services. Their handling of information is also governed by their own policies.

Retention

How long records remain

  • Resolved requests remain in Recent Requests for 30 days.
  • They then move to the staff-only Archive and are permanently deleted after six months there.
  • Pending and awaiting-payment requests remain active until they are resolved or expire.
  • Discord delivery-health logs older than 90 days are removed during system-health maintenance.
  • Public completed-stream entries retain the YouTube link as the permanent VOD record. Twitch links stop appearing 60 days after the recorded completion date.
  • Copies held by connected services follow those services’ own retention policies.

Your Choices

Control over your information

  • You may browse the game catalog without signing in.
  • The viewer note is optional.
  • You may sign out at any time.
  • A pending request may be cancelled through Request Status before approval.
  • You may contact ⁅𝐓𝐡𝐲𝐓☣︎𝐱𝐢𝐜𝐆𝐚𝐦𝐞𝐫⁆ with a privacy question or request concerning your game-request record.

Protection

How access is protected

Database access rules restrict viewers to their own records and reserve staff operations for the owner and verified moderators. Private keys, payment-service credentials, and Discord webhook addresses remain in protected server-side configuration rather than public website code. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but the system limits access and stored information to what is needed for the request workflow.
